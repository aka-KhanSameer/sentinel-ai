ABOUT THIS DEMO

Evidence first, with limits shown

Sentinel combines deterministic rules, document structure, and an optional local classifier. Risk scores prioritize review; they are not probabilities and cannot certify a file as safe.

Formats

Text, PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, PNG, JPEG, and WebP. Scanned PDF OCR and full visual analysis are not supported.

Privacy

Files are not stored. Only limited scan metadata may be retained per private browser session when PostgreSQL is configured.

Known evaluation limit

The Northwind Chinese X-12 payload remains undetected. Multilingual and false-positive coverage is incomplete.

Sentinel flags suspicious patterns for review. A clean scan does not certify content as safe.
